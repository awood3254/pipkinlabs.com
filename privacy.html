Privacy

Last updated 27 October 2026

This notice covers the pipkinlabs.com website. MochiBoost will have its own, fuller privacy policy when the app launches.

Who we are

Pipkin Labs Ltd is the data controller for this website. Contact us at hello@pipkinlabs.com.

What we collect

Very little. This site has no sign-up forms, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not set cookies.

  • Emails you send us. If you email us, we keep your message and address so we can reply. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests. We delete correspondence when it is no longer needed, and within two years at most.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider automatically records technical details such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to keep the site secure and working. These logs are kept for a short period by the host.
  • Fonts. Page fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Email us and we will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
